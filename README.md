# Travara

Travara is a comprehensive tourist safety and resilience mobile application. Designed to empower travelers with real-time safety monitoring, Travara ensures that you can explore normally while the app continuously tracks risk environments, system connectivity, and your exact location.

## Key Features

- **Offline Capabilities**: Full access to offline maps and cached safety data (hospitals, police stations, high-risk zones, and translation dictionaries).
- **Trusted Companion**: Share your live GPS location, safety status, and battery levels with trusted individuals. 
- **Safety Assistance**: Powerful emergency triggers including Auto-SOS on disconnection, hardware button SOS (rapid power button presses), and loud alarms.
- **Incident Timeline**: Track incidents progressively and see exactly what happens when you report feeling unsafe.
- **Dynamic Risk Assessment**: Real-time evaluation of surrounding environments and automated guidance to safer nearby locations.
- **System Monitoring**: Transparent communication about GPS precision, internet connectivity, and background location services.
